## 3. The 15-Minute Breach Compensation Ledger

If StaySphere Central Operations fails to acknowledge and provide an actionable resolution within 15 minutes of a P0/P1 ticket initiation:

1. **Automated ₹3,000 Goodwill Credit**: Instantly credited to the guest's StaySphere wallet for future bookings or in-stay dining reimbursement.
2. **24-Hour Escrow Payout Freeze**: Temporary hold placed on the disputed leg's vendor disbursement until resolution is fully accepted by the guest.
3. **Managing Director Notification**: High-priority alert sent to the executive leadership team.

---

## 4. Collaborative Incident Triage Protocol

1. **Incident Claiming**: The active dispatcher or RM claims the ticket in the Control Tower (`Workspace 4: SLA Resolution Queue`). This halts the initial response countdown clock.
2. **Cross-Party Coordination**: The RM communicates simultaneously with the Hotel GM, Fleet Lead, and Guest via the encrypted Collaborative Ticket Intercom.
3. **Resolution Action Execution**:
   - `DISPATCH_BACKUP_VEHICLE`
   - `AUTHORIZE_COMPLIMENTARY_UPGRADE`
   - `RELEASE_GOODWILL_CREDIT`
4. **Post-Mortem Logging**: Incident details, response time, and corrective action are logged into the permanent **360° Reputation Engine**.

## 5. Dispute Arbitration & Appeals

- If a partner disputes an SLA penalty or escrow hold, they may submit an appeal to the **Arbitration Board** via `compliance@staysphere.io` within 5 business days.
- The Arbitration Board reviews GPS telemetry, aviation radar logs, and recorded communications to deliver a binding resolution within 48 hours.
