## 1. Principles of Sovereign Trust

Trust is the foundational asset of StaySphere. High-net-worth travellers, luxury hoteliers, and executive fleet operators rely on our strict verification standards to ensure safety, privacy, and flawless service delivery.

## 2. Property Verification: The 84-Point Sovereign Audit

Before any property is granted the **"Sovereign Verified"** seal and activated for bookings, it must achieve a passing score of $\ge 95.0\%$ on our physical audit:

```
+-------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                  THE 84-POINT SOVEREIGN PROPERTY AUDIT                  |
+-------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| Category                        | Checks | Minimum Passing Threshold    |
+---------------------------------+--------+------------------------------+
| 1. Hygiene & Sterilization      | 24     | 100% Mandatory               |
| 2. Climate, HVAC & Lighting     | 18     | 95% (22°C Pre-Cooling Calib) |
| 3. Digital Locks & Physical Sec | 20     | 100% (AES-256 / BLE Keycard) |
| 4. Service Turnaround & Dining  | 22     | 90% (<25 Min In-Suite Dining)|
+---------------------------------+--------+------------------------------+
```

### Recurring Audit Protocol:
- **Bi-Annual Physical Re-Audit**: Unannounced quality inspections by Regional Hospitality Leads.
- **Guest Feedback Trigger**: If a property’s rolling 30-day Trust Score drops below **95.0%**, an immediate inspection is dispatched within 48 hours.

## 3. Mobility & Chauffeur Fleet Verification

Every vehicle and chauffeur in the StaySphere mobility fleet undergoes comprehensive vetting:

### 3.1 Driver Verification
- **Background Checks**: Comprehensive police background clearance and minimum 5 years of professional chauffeur experience.
- **Defensive Driving Certification**: Mandatory annual certification covering executive convoy driving and evasive security handling.
- **Language & Etiquette**: English and local language proficiency with strict passenger privacy non-disclosure agreements (NDAs).

### 3.2 Vehicle Standards
- **Age Limit**: Vehicle manufacture date must not exceed **36 months** (Mercedes-Maybach, Land Rover Defender, BMW 7-Series, Audi A8).
- **Daily Inspection**: Pre-trip telemetry check for tire pressure, brake pads, active 5G Wi-Fi, air purification filters, and pre-cooling system.

---

## 4. Guest & Channel Partner Verification (KYC)

### 4.1 Guest Identity Assurance
- All guest bookings are linked to verified government identification (Passport, Aadhaar, Driver's License) submitted digitally prior to suite lock provisioning.
- Diplomatic and high-profile guests may request **Anonymous White-Glove Check-In** via their dedicated Relationship Manager.

### 4.2 Channel Partner Accreditation
- Travel agencies and corporate concierge desks must provide valid IATA / Ministry of Tourism licensing and corporate credit backing before API keys are issued.

---

## 5. Payment Custody & Milestone Escrow Safety

- **100% Custody Segregation**: Guest funds are held in segregated, escrow accounts backed by Tier-1 Indian banking institutions.
- **No Float Speculation**: StaySphere never deploys escrowed funds for speculative operational float. Payouts are solely disbursed according to verifiable journey completion milestones.
