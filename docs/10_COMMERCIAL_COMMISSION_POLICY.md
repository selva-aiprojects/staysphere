## 2. Stakeholder-Specific Terms

### 2.1 Property Partners (Hotels, Resorts, Luxury Villas)
- **Standard Platform Fee**: 10.0% for HostSphere PMS integrated estates; 12.0% for external PMS connectors.
- **Payout Trigger**: Automated release from escrow **2 hours after guest check-in** is logged by the front desk.
- **Deductions**: Net payout equals Gross Booking Subtotal minus Platform Fee. GST on platform fee (18%) is invoiced separately with input tax credit (ITC) eligibility.

### 2.2 Travel & Mobility Partners (Chauffeur Fleets)
- **Standard Platform Fee**: 8.0%–10.0% of the base transit fare.
- **Tolls & Parking**: 100% of state border taxes, airport VIP parking tokens, and highway fastag tolls are reimbursed without deduction.
- **Payout Trigger**: Reconciled and transferred via RTGS/NEFT daily at 20:00 IST.

### 2.3 B2B Channel Partners (Agencies & Corporate Concierges)
- **Commission Tiers**:
  - `Silver Tier` (<₹10L/month): 10.0%
  - `Gold Prestige` (₹10L–₹50L/month): 12.5%
  - `Centurion / Black Tier` (>₹50L/month): 15.0%
- **Settlement Method**: Real-time net billing deduction or weekly direct payout.

## 3. Rate Parity Policy & Public Price Guarantee

StaySphere enforces strict rate parity to protect our travel ecosystem:
1. **Public Price Consistency**: Property partners must ensure room rates on StaySphere match or beat their public website and legacy OTA listings.
2. **Discrepancy Remedies**: If an unauthorized price markup is detected by the StaySphere crawler, the property receives an automated collaborative ticket. Continued violations result in temporary loss of the "Sovereign Verified" ranking boost.

## 5. Dispute Resolution & Escrow Holds

If a P0 incident occurs (e.g. room uninhabitable, severe delay), Central Operations may place a **24-Hour Temporary Escrow Hold** to investigate. Once resolved, funds are disbursed according to the formal resolution ruling.
