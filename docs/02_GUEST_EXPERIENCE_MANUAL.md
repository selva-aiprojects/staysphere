## 2. Searching & Booking a Complete Journey

### 2.1 The 4-in-1 Journey Planner
On the **StaySphere Home Page**, use the multi-mode search selector:
1. **Destination**: Select from Goa (Beaches & Cliffs), Udaipur (Heritage Palaces), Manali (Alpine Snow Chalets), or Mumbai (Sea-View Serviced Penthouses).
2. **Stay Category**: Choose between 5-Star Boutique Hotels, Private Pool Villas, Heritage Island Palaces, or Luxury Serviced Penthouses.
3. **Select Travel Dates & Nights**: View transparent real-time pricing with guaranteed rate parity.

### 2.2 Coupling Mobility & Airport Transfers
During the booking process:
- **Vehicle Selection**: Select between Mercedes-Maybach S680, Land Rover Defender 4x4, or Executive VIP EV.
- **Flight Synchronization**: Enter your inbound flight number (e.g. `6E-204`). StaySphere automatically links your flight telemetry to your chauffeur and hotel reception.
- **Curated Excursions**: Add private chartered speedboats, solar boat island cruises, or mountain snow safaris with 1 click.

### 2.3 Milestone-Protected Custody
- Your payment is held in the **StaySphere Sovereign Escrow Vault**.
- Funds are only disbursed to the hotel after you safely check in and are satisfied with your suite.
