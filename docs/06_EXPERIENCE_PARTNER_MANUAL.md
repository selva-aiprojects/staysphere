## 2. Experience Quality & Safety Audit Standards

Before an excursion is published to the StaySphere marketplace, our Regional Audit Team conducts an on-site inspection:

### 2.1 Marine & Speedboat Standards
- **Vessel Certification**: Valid mercantile marine department (MMD) license, twin-engine redundancy, onboard life-jackets, distress beacons, and marine GPS.
- **Onboard Inclusions**: Chilled champagne bucket, fresh local canapés, clean restroom, shade canopy, and certified rescue diver/first-aid responder onboard.

### 2.2 Mountain 4x4 Safari Standards
- **Vehicle Rigging**: High-clearance 4x4 (Land Rover Defender, Toyota Hilux), winter tire chains (Manali/Kashmir), recovery winch, emergency oxygen canister, and satellite communication radio.
- **Naturalist Certification**: Government-certified mountain guide with extensive knowledge of flora, fauna, and emergency route deviations.

### 2.3 Royal & Heritage Palace Tours
- **Historian Credentials**: Licensed royal palace historians offering skip-the-line VIP access, Mewari royal high tea setup, and artisan workshops.

## 3. Operations & Guest Coordination

### 3.1 Excursion Dispatch Lifecycle
1. **24 Hours Prior**: Experience partner receives the guest profile, dietary allergies, and special occasion notes (e.g. Anniversary Champagne).
2. **2 Hours Prior**: Live GPS radar confirms guest location and assigned transit chauffeur.
3. **15 Minutes Prior**: Captain / Lead Guide is stationed at the rendezvous point with welcome refreshments.
4. **Tour Execution**: Conduct itinerary at a relaxed, luxury pace. No rushing.
5. **Post-Tour Handover**: Escort the guest back to their awaiting chauffeur for the return hotel transit.

---

## 4. Milestone Payouts & Cancellation Safeguards

### 4.1 Automated Escrow Settlement
- Guest payments are held in the StaySphere Escrow Vault upon booking.
- Upon tour completion, the experience partner marks the session `COMPLETED` on their mobile console.
- Payout is automatically credited to the partner's registered bank account within **24 hours**.

### 4.2 Weather & Marine Disruption Safeguards
- If a boat tour or mountain safari is cancelled due to adverse weather or government maritime warnings:
  1. The partner flags `WEATHER_HOLD` in the console.
  2. Central Operations attempts to reschedule for the following morning.
  3. If rescheduling is impossible, the partner receives a **50% mobilization fee guarantee** from the StaySphere contingency vault, while the guest receives a 100% refund.
