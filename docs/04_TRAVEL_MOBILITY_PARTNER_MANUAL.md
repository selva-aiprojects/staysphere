## 2. Fleet Standards & In-Car Luxury Inclusions

All vehicles operating under the StaySphere Sovereign Mobility standard must strictly adhere to the following checklist before every dispatch:

### 2.1 Vehicle Inspection (Pre-Trip)
- **Exterior & Interior Hygiene**: Spotless exterior wash, pristine upholstery, and zero odors.
- **Climate Control**: Pre-cooled to **21°C–22°C** prior to passenger boarding.
- **In-Vehicle Amenities (Mandatory)**:
  1. Two chilled glass bottles of San Pellegrino / Himalayan Sparkling Mineral Water.
  2. Wrapped, chilled eucalyptus-infused linen towels.
  3. High-speed 5G Wi-Fi hotspot active.
  4. Multi-device charging station (Lightning, USB-C, MagSafe).
  5. Curated ambient background playlist (Lounge / Classical / Jazz) played at low volume.

### 2.2 Chauffeur Dress & Conduct Standards
- **Attire**: Dark tailored suit, pressed white collared shirt, tie, and polished black leather shoes.
- **Luggage Protocol**: Chauffeur must handle all baggage from airport carousel / curb to vehicle trunk and from vehicle to hotel concierge.
- **Discretion & Courtesy**: Polite greeting with name sign tablet; no unsolicited conversation or loud mobile usage while driving.

## 3. Flight Telemetry & Airport Pickup Protocol

### 3.1 Live Flight Synchronization
The StaySphere Operations Engine connects to live aviation radar:
1. **Flight Scheduled**: Trip appears in your dispatch queue with scheduled time.
2. **Flight In-Air**: Real-time GPS tracking of aircraft altitude and ground speed.
3. **Flight Touchdown**: System triggers an automated notification: `FLIGHT LANDED (GATE 04)`.
4. **Driver Stationing**: Chauffeur must be positioned at the airport VIP Lane / Arrivals Hall **15 minutes prior to estimated landing**.

### 3.2 Airport Meet & Greet
- Hold a digital iPad tablet displaying the guest's surname (e.g., `"MR. VIKRAM MALHOTRA"`).
- Escort the guest through the VIP exit lane directly to the awaiting vehicle.
- Tap **"Passenger Onboard"** in the Chauffeur Dashboard.

## 4. In-Stay Mobility, Yachts & Sightseeing Tours

### 4.1 Curated Day Excursions
When assigned to a Sightseeing or Day Tour (e.g., *Private Sunset Speedboat Tour in Goa* or *Lake Pichola Solar Boat in Udaipur*):
- **Timing**: Arrive at the hotel portico **10 minutes before the scheduled departure**.
- **Coordination**: Check in with the hotel concierge to ensure the guest's excursion kit (sun protection, champagne hamper) is aboard.
- **Skipper / Guide Protocol**: Certified naturalists and marine skippers must deliver professional commentary without rushing the itinerary.

## 5. Automated Payouts & Transit Escrow Settlement

### 5.1 Financial Workflow
- **Custody Lock**: The transit fare is held in StaySphere Escrow Vault upon booking confirmation.
- **Trip Completion Trigger**: Once the chauffeur marks the trip as `COMPLETED` at the destination, the guest is prompted for a 1-tap confirmation.
- **Disbursement**: Transit payouts are reconciled and transferred to the Fleet Operator’s bank account daily at **20:00 IST** with detailed trip-by-trip invoices.

### 5.2 Commission & Toll Fees
- Platform mobility fee: Strictly **8%–10%**.
- Airport parking fees, state highway tolls, and green corridor taxes are included in the fare calculations and reimbursed 100%.

## 6. Emergency Protocols & The 15-Minute SLA Guarantee

### 6.1 Mechanical Breakdown or Traffic Gridlock
If an en-route delay exceeds 10 minutes:
1. **Trigger Incident**: Tap `Report Delay` on the Fleet Console.
2. **Backup Vehicle Dispatch**: StaySphere Central Ops will immediately authorize a backup vehicle from the nearest standby hub within 8 minutes.
3. **SLA Compensation**: StaySphere’s Sentinel Engine will automatically credit the guest with an SLA compensation voucher, protecting the fleet operator’s partnership standing if delay was reported proactively.
